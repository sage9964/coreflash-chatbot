The problem

Presale inboxes don't triage themselves

Bulk and OEM inquiries arrive at every volume, from every kind of buyer — and sorting the real deals from the tire-kickers usually falls on whoever checks email first.

01

Volume gets buried

A 500-unit data center order and a one-off personal question land in the same inbox, read in whatever order they arrive.

02

Slow first response

By the time a real buyer hears back, they've often already gotten a quote from someone faster.

03

Nothing gets tracked

Inquiries that don't convert to a call today just disappear — no record to follow up on later.

How it works

Four steps, one conversation

The same logic every time, whether it ends in a booked call or a logged inquiry.

01

Gather

Asks for quantity, use case, and who you're buying for — a couple of natural questions, not a form.

02

Qualify

Checks business affiliation first, then weighs quantity and use case against it.

03

Book or log

Qualifying leads pick a real slot on the consultant's calendar. Everyone else gets logged for follow-up.

04

Hand off

The consultant sees priority, specs, and contact info at a glance — no digging through a thread.
